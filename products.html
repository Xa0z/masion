The Collection

Fifty things, carried well.

Maison is two counters under one roof — a perfume house and a cosmetic house. Everything below was opened, worn and argued over before it earned a place on the shelf. Prices are in Iraqi dinar and carry the size on the bottle, so nothing is a surprise at the counter.

Delivery is free in Erbil and on any order over 100,000 IQD; elsewhere in Kurdistan and Iraq it is a flat fee by city, paid in cash on delivery.
