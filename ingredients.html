Beauty, understood

We can tell you what is inside.

A counter is only as good as its advice. Most of what is sold as skincare is one or two working ingredients carried in a pleasant base — so the honest thing is to name them, say what they do, and say how long it takes. Below are the four that do most of the work in the Maison cosmetic house, and how we suggest using them.

None of this is medical advice. If something stings, burns or breaks you out, stop using it and come in — we would rather lose a sale than sell you the wrong bottle.

The working ingredients

  1. 01

    Vitamin C

    Brightness

    Evens tone and softens dark marks over weeks, not days. Wear it in the morning, under sunscreen — without the sunscreen you are undoing the work every afternoon. Our serum carries it at 30 ml, which is roughly three months of daily use.

  2. 02

    Hyaluronic Acid

    Water

    Holds water in the upper layers so skin looks full rather than tight. Apply it to damp skin and seal it with a cream — on dry air alone it has nothing to hold, which is why it can feel worse in an Erbil summer if you skip that second step.

  3. 03

    Collagen

    Bounce

    Supports the cushion under the surface. It is best as an overnight mask, two or three nights a week, rather than something you layer every day. Night Repair is where we put it.

  4. 04

    Urea

    Comfort

    For skin that cracks and flakes — hands, heels, elbows. It softens and holds moisture at once, which makes it the one ingredient here that fixes something visible within days rather than weeks.

In perfume, the same rule.

A fragrance is a structure, not a smell: the notes that greet you, the ones that hold the middle, and the base that is still there at the end of the day. Khair is oud, davana and pink pepper — oud warmed and opened rather than made heavier. Sceptre is amber and vanilla over dry cedar. Ask for either on paper first, then on the back of your hand, and give it twenty minutes before you decide.
